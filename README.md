**Ph.D. candidate at Beihang University** · M.S. in Computer Engineering, New York University

I work on multimodal foundation models, agentic reinforcement learning, and visual perception.

[Google Scholar][scholar] · [LinkedIn][linkedin]

## Research interests

- **Multimodal foundation models** — vision–language modeling, video generation, and teacher–student distillation.
- **Agentic RL** — agent post-training with GRPO and on-policy distillation, multi-step reasoning, and evaluation.
- **Visual perception** — pixel-level detection and segmentation of low-visibility objects in images and video.
